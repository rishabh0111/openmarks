Privacy policy

Last updated 18 October 2026.

Openmarks collects no data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Nothing about you, your bookmarks or the sites you visit is sent to the Openmarks project or to anyone else.

What is stored, and where

  • Your tiles are ordinary bookmarks in a folder you choose. They live in your browser's bookmarks, and they sync only if you use your browser's own bookmark sync.
  • Settings and pictures (your theme, wallpaper choice, an uploaded wallpaper, tile pictures and screenshots) are kept in the extension's local storage on your device. They never leave it.

Requests Openmarks makes

All requests go directly from your browser to the site named below. There is no Openmarks server in between.

  • Tile pictures. To show a picture for a tile, Openmarks fetches that site's page and images (its cover image or logo) from the site you saved.
  • Daily wallpaper, only if you turn it on: today's picture from Bing, from Wikimedia, or from the project's own daily feed of Unsplash photos (a small file published on GitHub Pages, with the photos loaded from Unsplash).
  • Web search uses your browser's own search engine, the same as searching from the address bar.

The sites above receive the usual information any web request carries, such as your IP address, and their own privacy policies apply.

Permissions

Openmarks asks for access to your bookmarks (to show and edit your tiles), to storage (for settings), to tabs and site access (to fetch tile pictures and take screenshots of sites you visit for their tiles), and to the context menu (for "Add to Speed Dial").

Open source

Openmarks is free software under the GPL-3.0. You can read every line of it on GitHub. Questions about privacy are welcome as an issue there.
