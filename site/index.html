Openmarks GitHub

Your bookmarks, front and center.

Openmarks is a free, open-source new tab page for Firefox and Chrome. It shows the sites you use as tiles, built from a bookmarks folder you already have, and it never asks for an account.

Built on your bookmarks

Your tiles live in an ordinary bookmarks folder, so they sync wherever your browser's bookmark sync already reaches. Folders inside it show up as tabs across the top.

Add a site in a second

Paste a link anywhere on the page and it becomes a tile. Or right-click on any page and choose "Add to Speed Dial".

Arrange it your way

Edit a tile's name or link, drag tiles into the order you like, and delete without worry: every delete can be undone.

Find anything with one key

Press / to search your tiles, or keep typing to search the web with your browser's own search engine.

Make it look like yours

Pick a theme: Openmarks (light or dark, with an accent colour taken from your wallpaper), Vapor, Cyberpunk or Retro. Turn on transparency to let your wallpaper show through.

A new picture every day, if you like

Set a plain colour, a gradient or your own photo as the wallpaper, or get a fresh daily picture from Bing, Wikimedia or Unsplash. Tiles can show a site's cover image, a sharpened logo or a screenshot.

Hands on the keyboard (or a controller)

Move between tiles with the arrow keys or Vim keys, open them with Enter, and press ? to see every shortcut. It works with a game controller too.

Private by design

Openmarks has no account, no ads and no tracking. It doesn't collect anything about you or the sites you visit. The only requests it makes beyond your browser are the ones you turn on: a daily wallpaper, and pictures for the tiles you saved. The code is GPL-3.0 and open on GitHub, so you can check all of this yourself.
